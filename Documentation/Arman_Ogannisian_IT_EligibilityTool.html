1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

When university students first discover the International Excellence Scholarship (IES), they often encounter dense institutional documentation and assume it is a traditional lecture-only exchange programme. Consequently, capable candidates fail to appreciate the essential Community Engagement pillar, or hesitate to apply due to uncertainty about their skills and eligibility.

The IES Applicant Assistant solves this problem by structuring student discovery into an intuitive, 5-stage journey:

  1. 01 Discover: Grasp the programme philosophy within 15 seconds.
  2. 02 Explore: Understand the 3 foundational pillars (Exchange Study, International Community, Community Engagement).
  3. 03 Check: Complete an interactive, multi-step Fit Checker assessing motivation and matching personal skills.
  4. 04 Learn: Review verified FAQs and clarify questions using the controlled AI Knowledge Assistant.
  5. 05 Apply: Seamlessly transition to the official Copernicus Berlin portal (copernicusberlin.org/en).

2. Key Implemented Features

🎯 Interactive Fit Checker

4-step questionnaire evaluating university enrollment, international study motivation, community willingness, and specialized skill areas with personalized track recommendations.

🤖 Controlled AI Assistant

Safe, zero-hallucination conversational prototype grounded strictly in approved Copernicus documentation with source citations and automatic fallbacks.

📊 Real-Time Analytics HUD

In-app developer drawer tracking key applicant funnel KPIs (start/completion rates, official portal clicks, session duration) in real time.

🛡️ Strict Factual Safeguards

Zero invented scholarship amounts, dates, or institutional claims. Clear visual badges distinguish verified facts from cycle-specific consultations.

3. UX Decisions & Brand Discipline

  • Authentic Brand Identity: Implemented Copernicus Berlin’s signature energetic orange (#F37021) and academic navy (#1A2A44) on warm editorial cream (#FDFBF7).
  • Mobile-First Architecture: Optimized for university students discovering the scholarship via mobile browsers or social media (TikTok, Instagram).
  • Progressive Disclosure: Prevents cognitive fatigue by presenting information in digestible steps rather than overwhelming walls of legal text.
  • Explicit Disclaimers: Prominently displays: “This tool is for informational guidance only and does not determine official eligibility. Please consult the official IES information before applying.”
