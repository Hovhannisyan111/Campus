# Factual Verification & Accuracy Check

---

### My Approach to Facts and Accuracy

In the challenge instructions, Copernicus Berlin emphasizes:
> *"Do not invent scholarship amounts, deadlines, partner universities, guaranteed career outcomes, statistics, or testimonials."*

To make sure my prototype adheres strictly to this rule, I conducted a factual audit before writing any code or copy. Here is what is officially verified vs what I intentionally left to official channels.

---

### 2. Information Left for Official Verification (Not Invented)

To avoid giving misleading information to students:

* **No Made-Up Scholarship Amounts:** Because funding and stipends depend on intake cycles and eligibility, I did not put any dollar/euro amounts. The prototype tells students to check official financial terms on `copernicusberlin.org/en`.
* **No False Deadlines:** Deadlines change with each intake. The prototype links directly to the official application page rather than hardcoding static dates.
* **No Specific Partner Universities Claimed:** The prototype describes university study in Berlin generally, without naming unverified university partners.
* **No Official Admission Decisions:** The Fit Checker is clearly labeled as an **informal self-assessment**. It explicitly reminds students:  
  *“This tool is for informational guidance only and does not determine official eligibility.”*

---

### Summary
By following these rules, the prototype acts as a helpful, honest orientation tool that builds trust with students and protects the integrity of Copernicus Berlin.
